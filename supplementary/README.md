## Embedding alignment

The six `alignment_matrix_*.csv` files hold a symmetric 8 × 8 matrix per
dataset. Entry $(i, j)$ is the **mutual $k$-nearest-neighbour alignment**
between foundation models $i$ and $j$: for each test sample, take the $k = 50$
nearest neighbours in each model's embedding space and compute the Jaccard
overlap of the two neighbour sets, then average over samples. The diagonal is
identically 1 and is omitted from the figures.

Centered kernel alignment (Kornblith et al., 2019) is an alternative
probe-free similarity measure; it operates on Gram matrices rather than
neighbourhood sets and is not computed here. Generated by
`notebooks/PRISM_alignment_figures.ipynb`.

## Probe family

Calibration is probe-dependent, so we repeat the full protocol with a
one-hidden-layer MLP of width 256 in place of logistic regression, across all
864 cells. `mlp_vs_linear_ranking.csv` reports the Kendall tau between the two
orderings per (dataset, fraction); `mlp_vs_linear.csv` holds the per-cell
metrics under both families. Generated by
`notebooks/PRISM_rebuttal_mlp_probe.ipynb`.

MLP configuration, since the paper specifies only the hidden width: one hidden
layer of 256 units with ReLU, Adam at learning rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4,
early stopping on validation loss with patience 15 and a 200-epoch cap. Data
splits, seeds, ECE definition and temperature-fitting procedure are identical
to the linear-probe protocol.
